About MANHWAS — stories in pictures, brought to more readers

Comics are one of the oldest ways humans have told stories — moments from real life and worlds that exist only in imagination, drawn into panels and read in a minute or an afternoon. They have been called the ninth art, and they have their own festivals and awards around the world.

In the last decade a new wave has grown faster than any other: manhwa from Korea and manhua from China — full-colour, vertical-scrolling serials released chapter by chapter, every week. The old idea that comics are only for children no longer holds: the subjects have widened, the audiences have grown up, and the stories now speak to adults as much as to anyone.

MANHWAS exists for those stories. We are a reading platform first: a clean, fast, distraction-free reader where a chapter opens instantly and keeps its place while you scroll. Around it we keep a curated catalogue of more than 2,000 manhwa and manhua series with full chapter lists, weekly rankings driven by real reader activity, a release calendar, and a directory you can browse by genre, status, author, or year.

We do not publish these works ourselves. Chapters are aggregated from public sources and served straight from their own CDNs — we organise, index, and present them so you can find what to read next and pick up exactly where you left off. Series pages carry the metadata that matters (synopsis, author, artist, genres, status, release rhythm), and every chapter link opens the actual chapter document, never a dead end.

Our reader keeps your library, ratings, and reading progress in your own browser, with an optional account if you want them synced across devices. No paywalls, no forced sign-ups, no downloads.

We love these stories and we want more people to find them. If something is missing, mislabelled, or broken, tell us — every report makes the catalogue better for the next reader.
