The Lost City

Decommisioned police officer Zhounan, is perplexed by a peculiar psychological experience. Upon contact with any object, he is able to perceive an individual's thoughts and capabilities. Evidently he finds himself entangled within the city he is in - the numerous legends within the Lost City cities. And those seemingly absurd legends hide surprising secrets. A weird world slowly opens its doors to us...
