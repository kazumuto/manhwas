Penguin Loves Mev

The English male Mev and the Korean female Penguin who had met through an online messenger are enjoying their successful marriage in England for three years, after a lovely honeymoon in Korea. The story of these two, who overcome whatever cultural obstacles they may face with love and affection, are sure to put a smile on anyones face. Note: The comics are actually based off of the author's married life with her husband Mev.
