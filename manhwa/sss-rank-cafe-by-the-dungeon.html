SSS-Rank Cafe by the Dungeon

After dying from overwork, the protagonist wakes up three years in the past. Determined to escape the grind, they open a cozy café in their grandmother's old shop—only to unlock a "Café Owner" class with magical coffee skills. But their peaceful life takes a turn when mysterious quests and dangers emerge.
