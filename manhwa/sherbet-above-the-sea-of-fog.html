Sherbet Above the Sea of Fog

Sherbet, a girl from a noble family, is forced to marry a man she has never met. She decides to escape this fate and joins a cruise that sails through the sea of mist, a place full of legends and secrets. There, she meets several interesting characters, such as a pirate captain, a runaway princess, a curious mermaid and a cruel empress. She also discovers that the cruise is actually a ghost ship that takes the souls of the dead to the next world. She will have to face various dangers and challenges to escape the ship and find her true love.
