Privacy Policy

MANHWAS (manhwas.site) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers this website only, not the third-party sites we link to.

What we collect

Kept in your browser only. Your library, chapter ratings, reading progress, reader width/theme preferences, and cached chapter metadata are stored in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device, are not uploaded, and disappear if you clear your browser data.

If you create an account. An account is optional and only needed to sync your library and progress across devices. We store the email address and display name you provide, a securely hashed password, and your synced library/progress entries; an avatar is optional. This is stored in our own storage (Cloudflare R2) and is never sold.

Comments and reports. If you post a comment or send a report we store the text, the thread or series it belongs to, the timestamp, and — if signed in — your display name. Comments and reports are public; do not include personal information in them.

Technical data. Our hosting provider processes standard request data — IP address, browser user agent, requested URL, timestamp — to deliver pages, keep the service available, and prevent abuse. We do not build personal profiles from it.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: serve pages, chapters, and images, and keep your place while reading.
  • To operate optional accounts: sign-in, library sync, and password resets.
  • To keep the site working and safe: rate limiting, abuse prevention, fault diagnosis.
  • To improve the catalogue: aggregate, anonymised usage statistics.
  • To show advertising, which keeps the site free.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to remember your reader settings, library, and sign-in session. Third-party advertising partners may set their own cookies or identifiers.

Third-party advertising

MANHWAS is free to read and funded by advertising. We work with third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense, and their partners. These companies may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings (myadcenter.google.com), review how Google uses partner-site data (policies.google.com), and opt out of many vendors at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers that make the site work — hosting and storage (Cloudflare) and advertising partners as described above — and where legally required. Comments and reports you post are public by design.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR in the EEA and UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Most reading data lives only in your browser — clearing browser storage removes it immediately. For account data, email us and we will export or delete it. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Legal bases. We process personal data to perform our contract with you (the service and your account), with your consent (optional features and advertising choices where required), for legitimate interests (security and improvement), and to comply with legal obligations.

Children's privacy

MANHWAS is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and security

Account data is kept while your account exists and deleted on request. Comments and reports are kept while the thread exists. Passwords are stored hashed, connections are encrypted, and access to stored data is restricted to what the service needs to operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The current version is always on this page.
