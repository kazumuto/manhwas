DMCA & Copyright

MANHWAS (manhwas.site) respects the intellectual property rights of others and takes copyright concerns seriously. We want to help creators, publishers, and rights holders manage how their work appears online while keeping the reading experience open and organised for fans.

What this site is — and what it is not

  • MANHWAS does not host, store, or upload chapter images or copyrighted files on its own servers. Chapter pages are served directly from the CDNs of the public sources that publish them.
  • What we operate is an index and a reader: metadata, chapter lists, search, rankings, and a reading interface. Series data is aggregated automatically from public sources.
  • We do not support piracy and we do not charge for access to any work.
  • Because aggregation is automatic, we cannot review every entry before it appears. If you believe something here infringes your rights, contact us and we will act on it.

Filing a DMCA notice

If you believe copyrighted material is accessible through this site in a way that constitutes copyright infringement, you may notify our designated copyright contact under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998. A valid notice must include all of the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list).
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it — the specific series and chapter URLs on manhwas.site. Links to search results or to another site do not identify an item and cannot be processed; list the individual titles/chapters you want removed, and if you link to another page, describe which material on it is under copyright.
  4. Information reasonably sufficient to let us contact you — an address, telephone number, and, if available, an email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate and that, under penalty of perjury, you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

How we respond

Valid notices are actioned promptly — typically by removing or de-listing the identified series or chapter entries from the catalogue and the reader, and by blocking the source entry from re-ingestion by future refreshes. A mistaken removal can be restored.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe the removal was the result of a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice with the same identifying details, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed in error, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court. We will forward valid counter-notices as required by law.

Send notices to

Email: manhwas@proton.me — put “DMCA” in the subject line and include the full details above. Incomplete notices (especially without specific series/chapter URLs) cannot be processed.

For anything that is not a legal notice — a broken chapter, a wrong chapter number, missing metadata — use the report box on any series page or write to the same address; those reports are read daily and usually fixed within a refresh cycle.
